For the grown-ups

This book is told from the baby’s side of the crib. Read it the way the day goes: bright and playful in the morning, softer and slower at bedtime. A baby doesn’t need a silent house. They need your voice, your face, and the same small rituals, repeated with love. That’s what “no shhh’s needed” means.
